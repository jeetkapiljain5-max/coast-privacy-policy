Coast — Support

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Coast scores how smoothly you ride. It records a ride on your phone, looks at how hard you braked and pulled away, and gives the ride a score out of 100 with coaching on what to change.

Recording only runs while Coast is open

This is the question we get most, and it is deliberate rather than a bug. Coast does not record in the background. If you leave the app mid-ride it stops the ride, saves what you rode up to that point, and posts a notification telling you it did. Nothing is lost, but nothing is recorded after that either.

To ride with the screen off, use Pocket Mode — the button below Stop Ride. It keeps Coast running with the screen dark.

Restoring a purchase

Open Settings in Coast and tap Restore purchase. That re-checks the store for a purchase already tied to your account, which is what you want on a new phone or after a reinstall.

If you are already Coast Pro the button is hidden, because there is nothing left to restore.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Coast cannot cancel a subscription for you — only the store can. On iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or tap Manage subscription in Coast's Settings. On Android, use Google Play › Subscriptions.

A lifetime purchase never renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Your ride history does not survive a reinstall

Rides are stored only on your phone. On Android, Coast switches automatic backup off on purpose so your rides are never copied to Google Drive — which also means uninstalling deletes them. Export anything you want to keep first, from Settings.

On iPhone, Apple includes Coast's folder in your device backup, so with iCloud Backup on your rides are part of that backup. See the privacy policy for the detail, including how to exclude Coast.

A score looked wrong

Coast judges a scooter and a car against different thresholds, because a car brakes far more gently than a two-wheeler. Coast asks which you are riding at the start of every ride — if you answer with the wrong one, the ride is scored against the wrong bar. There is no way to re-score a saved ride.

If a ride shows a dash instead of a score, Coast did not see enough of it to judge fairly — usually a very short ride, or one with a poor GPS fix.
